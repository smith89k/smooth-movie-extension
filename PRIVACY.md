# Smooth Movie – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Smooth Movie does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

## What the extension keeps

- **Your settings** (the on/off switches and the list of sites where you turned on "Close pop-up tabs on this site"). These are saved only in your own Chrome profile, using Chrome's built-in extension storage.
- **Block counts** (how many ads and pop-up tabs were blocked in each tab). These are kept in memory only while the tab is open and are deleted when you close the tab or Chrome.

None of this leaves your computer. The extension has no server, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

## Why it needs access to all websites

- **Blocking ads:** Chrome checks each web request against the extension's built-in list of ad networks and stops the matching ones. The extension does not read or save the pages or requests.
- **Closing pop-up tabs:** when a page opens a new tab, the extension compares the website names of the two tabs to decide whether to close the new one. Web addresses are not saved or sent anywhere.
- **Smoother playback:** a small script inside the page asks the page's video player to load more video ahead. It does not read what you watch or type.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

SMOOTH MOVIE - how to add it to Chrome
======================================

Add it (one time)
-----------------
1. Open Chrome.
2. In the address bar, type   chrome://extensions   and press Enter.
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (the switch in the top right corner).
4. Click "Load unpacked".
5. Pick this folder:   C:\_httdocs\smooth-movie-extension
6. Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then click the pin
   next to "Smooth Movie". Now its icon (a yellow play button) stays visible.

Use it on your movie site
-------------------------
1. Open the movie page.
2. Click the Smooth Movie icon.
3. Turn on "Close pop-up tabs on this site".
4. Click "Reload page" if it asks.

Ad blocking and smoother playback are already on for every site.
Click the icon to see how many ads were blocked and pop-up tabs were closed
in that tab.

Good to know
------------
- Chrome may show a note about "developer mode extensions" when it starts.
  That is normal for extensions you add yourself. Just close the note.
- If you change any files in this folder, go to chrome://extensions and click
  the round arrow on the Smooth Movie card to load the changes.
- The extension cannot make your internet faster. It saves more of the movie
  ahead of time, so short slowdowns don't stop the video.
- It helps most on free movie sites. Paid apps like Netflix lock their players,
  so it changes little there.
- When "Close pop-up tabs on this site" is on, links on that site that open
  OTHER websites in a new tab are closed too. Turn the switch off if you need one.
